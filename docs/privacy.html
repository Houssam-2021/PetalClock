Privacy information

Website: Petal Clock download page.

Website data

The interactive clock preview runs locally in your browser using JavaScript. It reads your device's local time to display sample clock digits. This website does not include advertising code, visitor analytics or account registration.

Google Fonts may be loaded from Google's servers if your browser allows it; requests to those services are governed by Google's privacy practices. The website links to the developer's other projects and includes downloadable source files.

Android app

Petal Clock does not collect, store or send any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics and no account. It reads your phone clock and battery level only to draw the wallpaper. The full source code is on GitHub.

Samsung Always On Display

Petal Clock does not replace Samsung's system-controlled AOD. It only draws through Android's live-wallpaper interface when that surface is available.
